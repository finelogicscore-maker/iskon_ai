Our Mission

Registered non-profit organisation

ISKCON AI is a non-profit organisation devoted to sharing the vani of His Divine Grace A. C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada—making authentic scripture study accessible through devotionally grounded AI, so seekers can hear, learn, and apply Krishna consciousness in daily life.

We build technology in the service of sankirtana: distributing books, supporting temples, caring for cows, and nourishing devotees and guests with prasādam. Every feature of this platform is meant to uplift consciousness, not replace the guidance of guru, sādhu, and śāstra.

  • RAG-based answers rooted in Prabhupada's books and ISKCON publications
  • Free spiritual inquiry for anyone drawn to Gaudiya Vaishnavism
  • Revenue directed back into seva—not personal gain

Mission commitment
